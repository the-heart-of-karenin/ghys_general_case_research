# Non-symplectic eight-dimensional holomorphic nil models

Date: 2026-10-20. Notes 108–109.

## Mathematical outcome

The h class is excluded without symplecticity: integrability first forces the entire four-dimensional center to be J-invariant, by the even-rank identity ad(Jz)J = Jad(Jz) and the rank 0/2/3 tensor-bracket geometry. On the quotient P+Q, the unit determinant and real-eigenvalue parity force the tensor action on the center to have unit-modulus spectrum. No semisimple replacement of the original J-preserving automorphism is made.

Combined with the three earlier exclusions that did not use Omega, the only possible real Lie algebra types of eight-dimensional holomorphic Anosov nil automorphism models are R8 and h3+h3+R2. Both types occur. Arbitrary invariant integrable complex structures, rather than preassigned left-invariant ones, are included via note60. This is a real Lie algebra type classification, not a classification of all lattices, complex structures, integer conjugacy classes, affine finite quotients, or arbitrary unknown holomorphic Anosov systems.
