## Proven boundary and unresolved step

The periodic unstable determinant target equals the obstruction MME = SRB. Failure leads to actual periodic orbits on both sides of the target rate, with no contradiction. Neither equality nor a new holomorphic quantitative estimate has been obtained. The compact real-analytic skew product matches all periodic characteristic polynomials and preserves smooth volume, but is not C1-diffeomorphically conjugate to its semisimple linear model. It cannot preserve any almost complex structure, so it does not refute the holomorphic problem.

The isolated scalar entry is paused, rather than repackaging general Gibbs estimates as progress on Ghys. A3 itself remains unresolved; future progress would have to use a genuine global integrable-complex-structure or derivative-matrix constraint.
