INVARIANT COMPLEX STRUCTURES FOR AFFINE AUTOMORPHISMS:
A COCYCLE VIEWPOINT
JIESONG ZHANG
Abstract.We prove that if a holomorphic diffeomorphism of a compact complex manifold
is bi-Lipschitz conjugate to an ergodic affine automorphismAon Γ\G, then the conjugacy is
C ∞. Moreover, ifAis weakly mixing, then the induced complex structure on Γ\Gis left-
invariant. As applications, we establish a regularity bootstrap result for holomorphic Anosov
diffeomorphisms bi-Lipschitz conjugate to affine models, as well as a holomorphic analogue of
the rigidity theorem for higher-rank abelian Anosov actions by Hertz–Wang [22].
The key observation is that the condition for a diffeomorphism to preserve a complex struc-
ture has the same form as the cocycle compatibility relation appearing in the study of central-
izers. This places invariant complex structures and centralizers within a commonZ 2-cocycle
framework. From this viewpoint, our main result may be regarded as a holomorphic coun-
terpart of the Lipschitz centralizer rigidity theorem of Damjanovi´ c–Wilkinson–Wu–Xu [5] for
affine automorphisms.
1.Introduction
An important problem in complex geometry is to determine which smooth real manifolds
admit complex structures, and to understand the space of all such structures. A classical and
still open example is the Hopf problem, which asks whetherS 6 admits a complex structure.
In holomorphic dynamics, it is natural to consider a dynamical analogue: given a smooth
diffeomorphismf:M→M, what are the complex structures onMfor whichfbecomes
holomorphic?
Problem1.1 (Invariant complex structures).LetMbe an even-dimensional smooth real mani-
fold admitting complex structures, i.e., the spaceJ M of complex structures onMis nonempty.
Letf:M→Mbe aC ∞ diffeomorphism. Describe the subset
JM(f)⊂ J M
consisting off-invariant complex structures onM, or equivalently, the space of complex struc-
tures onMwith respect to whichfis holomorphic.
This problem is particularly interesting when one imposes dynamical assumptions onf. For
instance, iff:M→Mis a diffeomorphism of a compact 4-manifold with positive topological
entropy, then any complex structure with respect to whichfis holomorphic must be K¨ ahler,
and the resulting complex surfaces can be further classified up to birational equivalence [4,
Theorem 10.1].
Another important case is given by Anosov systems. Ghys conjectured [12] that every holo-
morphic Anosov diffeomorphism is holomorphically conjugate to a holomorphic diffeomorphism
of a complex infra-nilmanifold. This conjecture was proved by Ghys [12] for complex surfaces,
and by Cantat [3] for projective varieties under the additional assumption that the stable and
unstable distributionsE s andE u are holomorphic. From the viewpoint of Problem 1.1, this con-
jecture can be formulated as follows: if an Anosov diffeomorphismf:M→Madmits an invari-
ant complex structureJ, then (M, J) should be biholomorphic to a complex infra-nilmanifold,
andfshould be biholomorphically conjugate to a holomorphic affine automorphism.
Problem 1.1 is challenging even in specific settings. For example, it remains open whether a
compact complex manifold diffeomorphic toT 2d,d≥3, and supporting a holomorphic Anosov
diffeomorphism must be a complex torus 1. This difficulty is also illustrated by an example of
1Ford≤2, this is always true by uniformization theorem and Kodaira classification.

Theorem A.Letf:X→Xbe a holomorphic diffeomorphism of a compact complex manifold
Xwith complex structureJ X. LetGbe a simply connected real Lie group, Γ< Gbe a

homeomorphismh:X→Γ\Gsuch that
h◦f=A◦h

•IfAis ergodic with respect tom, thenhisC ∞, and the push-forwardJ=h ∗JX belongs

particular,
h: (X, J X)→(Γ\G, J)
is biholomorphic, andAis holomorphic with respect toJ.
•Moreover, ifAis weakly mixing with respect tom, thenJ=h ∗JX is left-invariant.
In particular, by takingh= id Γ\G, we obtain the following corollary.
Corollary B.LetGbe a simply connected real Lie group, Γ< Gbe a cocompact lattice, and
mbe the Haar measure on Γ\G. For any affine automorphismA: Γ\G→Γ\G, letJ Γ\G(A)
denote the set of allA-invariant complex structures on Γ\G.
•IfAis ergodic with respect tom, thenJ Γ\G(A) is contained in a finite-dimensional

•IfAis weakly mixing with respect tom, thenJ Γ\G(A) consists of left-invariant complex

Remark1.5.Corollary B is not an existence statement. We do not assume that Γ\Gadmits
a complex structure, nor that it admits anA-invariant one. In particular,J Γ\G(A) may be
empty, in which case the conclusions are vacuous.
Moreover, the weak mixing assumption in Theorem A and Corollary B is essential. This
mirrors the centralizer rigidity theorem of [5]: whenAis ergodic but not weakly mixing with
respect tom, Walters constructed examples [24] of elementsf∈ Z ∞(A) which are not affine
(see also [5, Section 3]). This phenomenon also has an analogue in our setting, as shown in
Section 4.
Example C.In the setting of Corollary B, there exists an example for whichAis ergodic but
not weakly mixing with respect tom, andJ Γ\G(A) contains a complex structure that is not
left-invariant.

plied to study holomorphic Anosov diffeomorphisms. Recall that a diffeomorphismf:M→M
of a compact Riemannian manifoldMis calledAnosovif the tangent bundle admits a continuous

asserts that there are no other examples [23].

In general, a topological conjugacy need not beC 1, since the Lyapunov exponents at corre-
sponding periodic points may fail to coincide. Moreover, even when aC 1 conjugacy exists, it
need not beC ∞ [6, Theorem 6.3]. Nevertheless, for Anosov diffeomorphisms on tori that are
C1-conjugate to their linear models, the conjugacy is indeedC ∞ onT 2 [6,19] andT 3 [8,13,14].
More recently, Kalinin–Sadovskaya–Wang [15, 16] proved that if aC ∞ Anosov diffeomorphism
isC 1-conjugate to a very weakly irreducible hyperbolic toral automorphism, then the conju-
gacy isC ∞. For general nilmanifolds, although irreducibility conditions can be formulated to
study Lyapunov spectrum rigidity [7], no analogous bootstrap result seems to be known to the
author’s knowledge.
As a corollary of Theorem A, we get a holomorphic bootstrap result.
Corollary D.Letf:X→Xbe a holomorphic Anosov diffeomorphism of a compact complex
manifoldX. Suppose thatfis conjugate to an affine Anosov automorphismAof a real infra-
nilmanifoldNvia a bi-Lipschitz homeomorphismh:X→N. ThenNadmits a complex
infra-nilmanifold structureJ N such that
h:X→(N, J N)
is biholomorphic andA: (N, J N)→(N, J N) is a holomorphic affine automorphism.
Remark1.6.The holomorphic setting leads to a somewhat different phenomenon: no irreducibil-
ity assumption is needed for the bootstrap. Moreover, the corollary applies to affine models on
general infra-nilmanifolds, not only to toral models.
1.4.An application to Anosov actions.Theorem A can also be applied to study holomor-
phic higher-rank Anosov actions. Recall that an action
α:Z k →Diff ∞(M), k≥2,
is called anAnosov actionifα(n) is an Anosov diffeomorphism for somen∈Z k. WhenM
is homeomorphic to an infra-nilmanifold, the results of Franks [10] and Manning [18], together
with the commutativity of the action and a result by Walters [25], imply thatαis topologically
conjugate to an affine action

ofα.

an infra-nilmanifold and the linearizationρhas no rank-one factor 2, thenαisC ∞-conjugate to
ρ. In particular,Mis diffeomorphic to an infra-nilmanifold.

theorem.

1.5.Further questions.The relationship between Problem 1.1 and Problem 1.2 suggests that
conjectures and theorems concerning centralizers and abelian actions should have holomorphic
analogues. We give two examples here.
A guiding principle in the centralizer problem is that a large centralizer should force rigidity.
One of the prominent conjectures reflecting this philosophy is the Katok–Spatzier conjecture.
Conjecture1.8 (Katok–Spatzier).Letf∈Diff ∞(M) be an Anosov diffeomorphism. Suppose
that theC ∞-centralizerZ ∞(f) is higher-rank. ThenMis diffeomorphic to an infra-nilmanifold,
andfisC ∞-conjugate to an infra-nilmanifold automorphism.
In view of the analogy between centralizers and invariant complex structures, the natural
holomorphic counterpart of the Katok–Spatzier conjecture is Ghys’ conjecture on holomorphic
Anosov diffeomorphisms.
Conjecture1.9 (Ghys).Letf∈Diff ∞(M) be an Anosov diffeomorphism. Suppose that
JM(f)̸=∅, i.e.fpreserves at least one complex structure. Then for everyJ∈ J M(f),
the complex manifold (M, J) is biholomorphic to a complex infra-nilmanifold, andfis biholo-
morphically conjugate to an infra-nilmanifold automorphism.
On the other hand, trivial centralizers could be typical. A major problem in the study of
centralizers is Smale’s conjecture, which predicts thatC r-generically the centralizer is triv-
ial, i.e.,Z r(f) =⟨f⟩. This conjecture was proved in theC 1-topology by Bonatti–Crovisier–
Wilkinson [2]. Motivated by the analogy above, one may ask for the following analogue of
Smale’s conjecture for invariant complex structures.
Question1.10 (An analogue of Smale’s conjecture for invariant complex structures).LetMbe
a closed smooth manifold admitting at least one complex structure. Does there exist aC 1-open
andC ∞-dense subsetU ⊂Diff ∞(M) such that
JM(f) =∅
for everyf∈ U? Equivalently, does everyf∈ Ufail to preserve any complex structure onM?
Remark1.11.As shown by Bonatti–Crovisier–Vago–Wilkinson [1], the propertyZ r(f) =⟨f⟩is
notC 1-open. Thus in the centralizer problem, one can at best expect such a property to hold
generically, rather than on aC 1-open set.
By contrast, the question above has a straightforward answer in theC 1-open andC 1-dense
setting. Indeed, by theC 1 closing lemma [21] and Franks’ lemma [11], one can perturb a
diffeomorphism so as to create a periodic point whose return map has a real simple eigenvalue.
This is incompatible with the existence of anf-invariant complex structure.
