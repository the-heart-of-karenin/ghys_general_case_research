OBSTRUCTION THEORY ON 8-MANIFOLDS
MARTIN ˇCADEK, MICHAEL CRABB AND JI ˇR´I V ANˇZURA
Abstract. This paper gives a uniform, self-contained, and fairly dire ct approach
to a variety of obstruction-theoretic problems on 8-manifo lds. We give necessary
and suﬃcient cohomological criteria for the existence of co mplex and quaternionic
structures on 8-dimensional vector bundles and for the redu ction of the structure
group of such bundles to U(3) by the homomorphism U(3) → O(8) given by the Lie
algebra representation of PU(3).
1. Introduction
Let M be a connected, closed, smooth, 8-dimensional, spin c manifold and let ξ be
an 8-dimensional oriented real vector bundle over M admitting a spin c-structure. We
consider, for various compact Lie groups G and homomorphisms ρ : G → SO(8), the
problem of reducing the structure group of ξ from SO(8), via ρ, to G. In each case we
shall obtain, in terms of the cohomology of M and cohomology characteristic classes of
ξ and M, necessary and suﬃcient conditions for the reduction. The h omomorphisms
ρ that we examine are:
(i) the standard inclusion U(4) ⊆ SO(8);
(ii) the homomorphism U(3) → SO(8) determined by the adjoint representation of
the quotient PU(3) of U(3) by its centre;
(iii) the compositions of the standard inclusions Sp(2) ×{±1}U(1) ⊆ U(4) ⊆ SO(8);
(iv) the standard inclusion Sp(2) ×{±1}Sp(1) ⊆ SO(8);
(v) the composition of the deﬁning map and the standard inclu sion Spin c(k) →
SO(k) ⊆ SO(8) for k = 6, 5, 4, 3.
The results concerning almost complex structures on M (case (i) for ξ = τ M, the
tangent bundle of M) were obtained, using other methods, by Heaps [15] and by
Thomas [17]; see also Geiges and M¨ uller [14]. In [4], [5], [6 ], [8] ˇCadek and Vanˇ zura
dealt with (i) for spin vector bundles and with special cases of (iii) and (iv). The
result in case (ii) on reduction to U(3) through the adjoint r epresentation of PU(3)
was obtained by Crabb in response to a question of N.J. Hitchi n and F. Witt; see [18].
Conditions for existence of a 4-ﬁeld, (v), are also due to ˇCadek and Vanˇ zura [7].
